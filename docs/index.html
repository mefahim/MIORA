Designed for your personal library

MIORA is built for people who own their music. No streaming subscriptions, no remote servers, and zero telemetry.

Pure Local Library

Direct Android MediaStore integration indexes your music without uploading metadata. Browse by Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders, and Genres seamlessly.

Offline First

Resilient Audio Engine

AndroidX foreground playback service ensures uncompromised background listening with full audio focus handling, lockscreen controls, and system transport notification.

MediaPlayer / MediaSession

Product Philosophy

Music is personal. Your player should be too.

MIORA is being built around a simple idea: listening to your own music should feel intuitive, comfortable, and personal.

No Algorithms, No Subscriptions

Your music library belongs to you. MIORA will never upsell subscriptions, inject sponsored recommendations, or track what you play.

Respecting Device Resources

Engineered natively in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Clean lifecycle management and efficient background threading ensure minimal memory footprint and battery drain.

Transparent & Open Source

Licensed under Apache 2.0. Every line of code, release commit, and build script is publicly accessible on GitHub for peer review and security inspection.
